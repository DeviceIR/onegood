# LEGAL REQUIREMENTS TO VERIFY

These are **not** technical assumptions. Confirm with a qualified advisor before public fundraising (Phase 11).

## Organization

- [ ] Legal status of the operating entity
- [ ] Charity / NGO / institution registration (if required)
- [ ] Permissions for public online fundraising in Iran
- [ ] Bank account ownership in the organization's name
- [ ] ZarinPal merchant / terminal eligibility for charity use

## Finance & tax

- [ ] Accounting and tax treatment of donations
- [ ] Donation receipt obligations
- [ ] Financial reporting cadence and auditor access
- [ ] Who absorbs gateway fees (org vs donor)

## Privacy & children

- [ ] Privacy policy and terms of use published
- [ ] Consent for publishing children's photos / videos / stories
- [ ] Minimization of school / address / identifying data
- [ ] Data retention and deletion-request process
- [ ] Donor contact data purpose limitation

## Separation

| Technical (we build) | Legal (external confirmation) |
| --- | --- |
| Append-only ledger, audit log | Right to fundraise publicly |
| Consent publish gate | Valid guardian consent forms |
| Privacy toggles for donors | Lawful basis for processing |
| Secure payment verify | Merchant / bank eligibility |

**Do not assume a personal bank account is sufficient for public charitable fundraising.**
